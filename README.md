## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/WUlplcMpOCEmTGBtBW/giphy.gif" width="30"/> About Me

<img align="right" width="260" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExcDVsdnQ5MWo0cHR2NzlpZm9yeTR5cnJjbG1sOHprZWZiMHd4dHZkNyZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif"/>

I'm an **AI Researcher** based in **Würzburg, Germany** :de:, working at the **Technology Transfer Center Kitzingen (TTZ-KT)** — a THWS initiative bringing AI, robotics and digital transformation to regional industry. I started in **mechanical engineering** before moving into AI, which is why I think about hardware constraints and deployment from day one.

🔭&nbsp; Working on **LLM-powered solutions** and **RAG systems** for industrial applications
👁️&nbsp; Building **computer vision** pipelines for quality control and real-time detection
⚡&nbsp; Deploying deep learning on **edge hardware** — NVIDIA TAO, TensorRT, Jetson
🎓&nbsp; **M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence** @ THWS · **B.Tech Gold Medalist** @ GPREC
✍️&nbsp; I write about deep learning and edge inference on [**Medium**](https://medium.com/@korinetharunkumarpalli)
🤝&nbsp; Open to collaborating on **AI/ML research and open-source projects**
